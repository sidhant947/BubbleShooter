Bubble Shooter is a modern, colorful 2D bubble popping puzzle game. Aim, bounce off walls, and match 3 or more bubbles of the same color to clear the board and level up.

Key Features:

Classic Bubble Shooter Gameplay: Intuitive aiming and shooting physics. Match 3 or more bubbles to pop them.

Campaign Mode: Advance through progressively challenging levels designed to test your strategy and precision.

Zen Mode: Relax with endless, stress-free bubble popping without time or move pressure.

Custom Themes and Skins: Personalize your visual experience with distinct color palettes and styles.

Tactile Haptic Feedback: Feel satisfying response with every bubble pop and shot.

Completely Offline: Play anywhere, anytime without requiring an internet connection. Progress is automatically saved on your device.
